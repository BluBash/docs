The **Templates & Quick Replies** page lists everything you can see: templates from your channels and quick replies from your teams. There, you search, edit, and delete ready-made messages.

## The list

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Name of the template or quick reply. |
| **Type** | **Template** or **Quick Reply**. |
| **Category** | Template category. |
| **Status** | Templates: **Pending Approval**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Partial**, or **Inactive**. Quick replies: **Active** or **Inactive**. |
| **Channels/Teams** | Template channels or quick reply teams. Hover to see the status on each channel. |
| **Created at** | Creation date. |
| **Actions** | Buttons to edit and delete. |

Use **Search templates and quick replies...** to filter the list. Click **Refresh** to reload approval statuses.

## Who can edit and delete

| Role | Permission |
| --- | --- |
| **Administrator** | Edits and deletes any template or quick reply. |
| **Manager** | Edits and deletes items from the channels and teams they can access. |
| **Member** | Cannot edit or delete. Only uses them in conversations. |

To edit or delete, you need access to all channels or teams associated with the item. Otherwise, the buttons are disabled with the message **You need to have access to all teams/channels of this template to manage it**.

## Edit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the pencil icon">
    In the **Actions** column, click **Edit** on the item row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the content">
    The wizard opens directly at the **Edit** step, with the current data. The type and category chosen at creation do not change. Change the text, media, buttons, or variables and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the associations">
    Add or remove channels (template) or teams (quick reply). Keep at least one selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    In the **Review** step, click **Update Template**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### When a template cannot be edited

The edit button is disabled with the message **Cannot edit this template** when the template is not yet synced with WhatsApp on every associated channel. In that case, wait for the sync or create a new template.

<Note>
  Template changes follow Meta's rules. After editing an approved template, track the **Status** in the list. If the change alters the purpose of the message, create a new template instead and keep the old one until the new one is approved.
</Note>

## Delete

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the trash icon">
    In the **Actions** column, click **Delete** on the item row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The **Delete Template** window shows **Are you sure you want to delete this template? This action cannot be undone.** and **This template will be removed from all channels and teams where it is being used.** Click **Delete Template**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deletion cannot be undone. Before deleting a template, make sure it is not in use in an active campaign, especially an **Ongoing Campaign**.
</Warning>

## Best practices

- **Review the list regularly.** Delete quick replies nobody uses and rejected templates you will not fix.
- **Standardize names.** Prefixes such as `sales_`, `support_`, and `billing_` make search easier.
- **Update together with the AI agent.** If information changes, such as a price or business hours, update the quick reply and the AI agent training on the same day.
- **Test after editing.** Send the message to an internal contact and check variables, media, and buttons.
