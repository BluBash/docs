Templates are Meta-approved messages for the **WhatsApp Business API**. You use a template to start conversations, resume contact after 24 hours, and run campaigns. On this page, you create the template and submit it for approval.

<Note>
  Templates exist only for **WhatsApp Business API** channels. WhatsApp Lite, Instagram, and Webchat do not use templates. For ready-made messages on those channels, [create a quick reply](/templates/create-quick-reply).
</Note>

## Before you start

- Have at least one active **WhatsApp Business API** channel. See [Configure the WhatsApp Business API →](/channels/configure-whatsapp-official).
- You must be an **Administrator** or a **Manager**. Members only use templates in conversations.
- Define the goal of the message before writing. This points to the right category and lowers the risk of rejection.

## Wizard steps

The creation wizard has five steps: **Setup**, **Category**, **Edit**, **Associations**, and **Review**. The bar at the top shows your progress.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Templates & Quick Replies">
    Go to **Settings** and click **Templates**. On the **Templates & Quick Replies** page, click **Create Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the message type">
    In **Choose message type**, click **Select Template** on the **Message Template** card, marked **Requires approval**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the category">
    In **Choose a template category**, select the option that describes the message. Two BluBash options are mapped automatically to Meta categories, shown by **→ WhatsApp Category:**.

    | Option in BluBash | Category sent to Meta | When to use |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Marketing** | Marketing | Promotions, offers, newsletters, campaigns |
    | **Utility** | Utility | Order confirmation, delivery status, reminders, notifications |
    | **Authentication** | Authentication | Verification codes, login confirmations, security alerts |
    | **Customer Service** | Utility | Questions, scheduling, support, resuming conversations |
    | **Sales** | Marketing | Product presentations, quotes, sales follow-up |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the template">
    In the **Create your template** step, fill in the fields described below, in the **Template fields** section. The **Preview** on the side shows how the message will look to the contact. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate channels">
    In **Select Channels**, check the channels where the template will be available. The list shows only active **WhatsApp Business API** channels. Select at least one channel. The template is submitted for approval on each selected channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and create">
    In **Review Template**, check the **Final Preview**, the **Template Information**, and any **Warnings**. Click **Create Template**. The template goes to Meta with the **Pending Approval** status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Template fields

### Basic Information

| Field | Required | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Template Name** | Yes | Use a descriptive name. BluBash converts it to lowercase and replaces spaces with `_`. Example: "Appointment Reminder" becomes `appointment_reminder`. |
| **Language** | Yes | **Portuguese (Brazil)**, **English (US)**, or **Spanish**. Choose the language the text is written in. |

### Header

The **Header** is optional. In **Header Type**, choose:

| Type | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| **No Header** | The message starts with the body. |
| **Text** | Up to 60 characters. Accepts 1 variable, always `{{1}}`. |
| **Image** | JPG, PNG, GIF, or WEBP, up to 5 MB. |
| **Video** | MP4, AVI, MOV, or WMV, up to 16 MB. |
| **Document** | PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT, CSV, XLS, XLSX, PPT, or PPTX, up to 10 MB. |

<Tip>
  Meta is stricter with header media. To avoid rejection, prefer JPG or PNG images, MP4 videos, and PDF documents.
</Tip>

### Body

The **Body** is required and has up to 1,024 characters. It is the main text of the message. Use **Add Variable** to insert `{{1}}`, `{{2}}`, and so on, up to 10 variables. See [Use variables →](/templates/use-variables).

### Footer

The **Footer** is optional and has up to 60 characters. Use it for a signature or a short notice, such as "Delivery Team".

### Buttons

**Buttons** are optional. Click **Add Button** to include up to 10 buttons.

| Button type | What it does | Fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick Reply** | The contact taps it and replies with the button text. The reply reopens the 24-hour window. | Button text |
| **URL** | Opens a link. | Button text and button URL |
| **Phone** | Calls a number. | Button text and phone number |

- Each button text has up to 25 characters.
- Do not interleave types. Group the **Quick Reply** buttons on one side and the **URL** and **Phone** buttons on the other. Valid examples: Quick Reply, Quick Reply, URL, Phone; or URL, Phone, Quick Reply, Quick Reply.

### Variable Examples

If the template has variables, fill in sample values in **Variable Examples**. They are used only for the preview and are not sent to the contact.

## Approval status

After you create it, the template appears in the list with a status. Click **Refresh** to reload the list.

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pending Approval** | Meta is reviewing the template. | Wait. Meta sets the review time. |
| **Approved** | The template can be used in conversations and campaigns. | Use it normally. |
| **Rejected** | Meta declined the template. | Hover over the status to see the **Reason**. Adjust it and create a new template. |
| **Partial** | The status differs across the associated channels. | Hover over the **Channels/Teams** column to see the status of each channel. |
| **Inactive** | The template was disabled. | Use another template or create a new one. |

## Best practices for approval

- **Choose the right category.** A promotional message marked as Utility is often rejected or recategorized by Meta.
- **Do not start or end the body with a variable.** Put text before and after it.
- **Give variables context.** `Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} has shipped` is clearer than `{{1}} {{2}}`.
- **Check spelling and links.** Typos and shortened URLs increase the risk of rejection.
- **Make the sender clear.** Mention your company name in the text.
- **Create templates to resume conversations.** Keep at least one Utility or Customer Service template for team members to use when a lead stops replying to the AI agent or to the team.

## Full example

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | Customer Service (→ Utility) |
| **Template Name** | `resume_conversation` |
| **Language** | English (US) |
| **Header** | Text: "Your request" |
| **Body** | `Hi {{1}}, this is the Example Store team. Do you still have questions about your order? We can continue here.` |
| **Footer** | "Example Store" |
| **Buttons** | Quick Reply: "Continue"; Quick Reply: "No, thanks" |

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The channel list is empty">
    **No active channels available** appears when there is no active WhatsApp Business API channel. Check the channel in **Channels**. WhatsApp Lite, Instagram, and Webchat channels do not appear because they do not use templates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Invalid button order">
    You interleaved **Quick Reply** buttons with **URL** or **Phone** buttons. Reorder them so that buttons of the same group stay together.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The media file is not accepted">
    Check the format and maximum size for the selected type in the header table.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The template was rejected">
    Read the **Reason** on the status. The most common causes are a wrong category, a variable at the start or end of the text, and promotional content in a Utility template.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
