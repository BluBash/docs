BluBash AI agents serve your customers on your channels: WhatsApp, Instagram and Webchat. They understand messages, answer based on what you taught them, take actions such as adding notes and applying tags, and resolve routine requests on their own. When a conversation needs a person, the agent transfers it to the right team with the context recorded.

## How an AI agent works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You create the agent">
    You define the name, purpose, tone, behavior and company information in the creation wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You teach the agent">
    In the **Trainings** tab, you add text, files and your company website. The agent answers based on this content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You define what it can do">
    In the **Skills** tab, you turn on native skills and set up tags, transfers, media sending and deal management in Pipelines. In the **Apps** tab, you connect external APIs and tools such as Google Calendar, Asaas and RD Station. In the **Settings** tab, you adjust the conversation style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You test">
    In **Test Agent**, you simulate conversations and check the answers and the skills executed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You link the agent to channels">
    In **Channels**, you choose the agent in the **AI Agent (Optional)** field of each channel. New conversations on that channel are then handled by the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent and the team work together">
    The agent resolves routine requests and transfers the rest. The team hands the conversation back to the agent with **Handoff to AI** and takes it back with **Take Over**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where the agent works

| Channel | How the agent takes part |
| --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp** (official WhatsApp Business API or WhatsApp Lite) | Handles conversations that reach the linked number. It is the main channel for most operations. |
| **Instagram** (Direct) | Answers messages received in the Direct inbox of the connected account. The channel is inbound only. |
| **Webchat** | Answers visitors who open the chat on your website. The channel is inbound only. |

Each channel has at most one linked AI agent. The same agent can be linked to several channels: use a single agent for WhatsApp, Instagram and Webchat, or create specific agents for each channel.

## Agent tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Purpose, tone, behavior and company information |
| **Trainings** | Content the agent uses to answer: text, file and website |
| **Skills** | Native skills and configured actions: tags, transfers, media and Pipelines |
| **Apps** | External APIs and ready-made integrations with external tools |
| **Settings** | Response style, voice response, topic restriction and timezone |

The **Test Agent** button, in the top right corner of the agent page, opens the test chat.

## Available purposes

| Purpose | Use case |
| --- | --- |
| **Sales** | Lead qualification, presenting services and routing to Sales |
| **Support** | Solving questions and issues about products and services |
| **Customer Service** | General assistance and routing to teams |
| **Lead Generation** | Capturing and qualifying potential customers |

## Who can manage agents

Only the **Administrator** role creates, configures, activates, deactivates and deletes AI agents. **Manager** and **Member** work on the conversations handled by agents, including handing conversations off to AI and taking over conversations from AI.

[Roles and permissions →](/members/roles-permissions)

## Limits and precautions

- The number of agents active at the same time depends on your plan.
- Trainings and voice responses consume AI credits.
- The agent answers based on what it was taught and configured. Review the training whenever products, prices or procedures change.
- The AI agent only acts in the Pipelines and stages you unlocked in the **Manage deals** skill. Review the skill whenever you change the stages.

<Tip>
  Start with an agent that answers frequent questions and routes to the team. Adjust the training based on real conversations and gradually increase what it resolves on its own.
</Tip>
