BluBash is an AI-powered conversational CRM. AI agents serve your customers around the clock on WhatsApp, on Instagram and in the chat on your website. They handle routine requests, qualify leads and organize every conversation. Your team takes over when someone needs to negotiate, decide or analyze, and follows each customer in Pipelines until the process is complete.

## Service channels

Every channel lands in the same **Conversations** inbox. Your team replies from one place, and the AI agent can serve on any of them.

| Channel | How it connects | Who starts the conversation | What to keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp Business API** | Meta's official API | The contact or your team | Outside the 24-hour window, only with an approved template. Required for campaigns. |
| **WhatsApp Lite** | QR code, from WhatsApp on your phone | The contact or your team | Set up in minutes, for low volume. Unofficial, with no stability guarantee. |
| **Instagram** | Official API, with the professional account login | Only the contact, through Direct | You can reply within 7 days of the contact's last message. |
| **Webchat** | Widget installed on your website | Only the visitor | Conversations start from the widget. |

<Note>
  Telegram and Email appear on the channels screen as **Coming soon**.
</Note>

## How the features connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="The customer reaches out on one of your channels">
    The message arrives through WhatsApp, Instagram Direct or the website widget and lands in the inbox of the channel's default team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The AI agent answers first">
    If the channel has an AI agent linked, it answers questions, qualifies the lead, adds a summary note and applies tags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The team takes over when needed">
    The agent transfers to the right team. The team member reads the history and notes and continues the conversation. When the human part is done, they can hand the conversation back to the AI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The process becomes a deal">
    The team member creates the deal in a Pipeline from the conversation itself and tracks stages, tasks and deadlines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The next area receives it automatically">
    When the deal is won, derivation creates a new deal in the next area's Pipeline, such as Onboarding or Legal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The manager tracks the numbers">
    Reports show volume, satisfaction and how much the AI handled on its own. The AI analysis scores the quality of each conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who BluBash is for

BluBash serves operations that sell to, support or follow up with customers through messaging: sales teams, support, customer success, collections and onboarding. It works for one person handling a few conversations a day and for several teams handling thousands, because distribution, roles and permissions are set per team.
