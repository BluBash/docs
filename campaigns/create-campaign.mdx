The campaign wizard guides you step by step, from the campaign type to the final review. When you finish, you can save it as a draft, start it right away, or schedule it.

<Note>
  Only **Managers** and **Administrators** create campaigns. Campaigns are sent only through WhatsApp channels. Read the [Campaigns introduction →](/campaigns/campaigns-introduction) first to understand Meta's rules.
</Note>

## Before you start

- Have an active WhatsApp channel. For sends to your general base, use the **WhatsApp Business API (official)**.
- On the official API, create and get approval for the template you will send. See [Create a template →](/templates/create-template).
- Organize contacts with tags and additional fields to filter the audience.
- Confirm that contacts opted in to receive messages from your company.

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wizard">
    In the sidebar, click **Campaigns** and then **New Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the campaign type">
    | Type | When to choose it |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Single Campaign** (Batch) | One-off send to an audience defined now, immediate or scheduled. |
    | **Ongoing Campaign** (Automation) | Automatic send to contacts who join the campaign over time, manually, through the API, or through RD Station Marketing. |

    Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the campaign details">
    | Field | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Campaign name** | Short name to identify the campaign. Up to 100 characters. |
    | **Description (optional)** | Campaign goal for your team. |
    | **Sending speed** | Messages sent per minute, from 1 to 300. The default is 60. |
    | **Send now** or **Schedule** | Single Campaign only. In **Schedule**, choose the **Date and time**. You cannot schedule in the past or more than 365 days ahead. |

    <Warning>
      Very high sending rates may result in channel restrictions. Adjust the speed to the audience size and your number's limits.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the sending channel">
    Choose the WhatsApp channel that will deliver the campaign. Only channels you are allowed to use appear. Make sure the channel is active.

    If **No WhatsApp channels available** appears, create a channel or ask the Administrator for access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the audience (Single Campaign)">
    Combine filters to reach the right contacts. The **Audience preview**, on the right, shows how many contacts are selected and sample contacts.

    | Filter | How it works |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Tags** | Selects contacts with the chosen tags. |
    | **Filter by conversation status** | **All**, **Open only**, or **Closed only**. |
    | **Last interaction** | Period of the last interaction: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **This month**, **Last month**, or **Custom date**. |
    | **Field Filters** | Filters by **Default Fields** (Name, Email, Phone) or **Additional Fields**, with conditions such as **Equals**, **Not equals**, **Contains**, **Not contains**, **Starts with**, **Ends with**, **Is empty**, and **Has value**. |

    You must apply at least one filter, and the audience must have at least one contact to continue.

    <Info>
      The Ongoing Campaign skips this step. It does not use a fixed list: you add contacts after it is created.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how you will communicate">
    | Format | When to use it |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Template** | Uses a saved template. On the official API, choose a template approved by Meta to reach contacts outside the 24-hour window. |
    | **Manual text** | Free message personalized with variables. On the official API, it is delivered only to contacts who interacted in the last 24 hours. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose the message">
    **If you chose Template:**

    1. In **Use an approved template**, search for and select the template. Filter by **All**, **Templates**, or **Quick replies**.
    2. In **Variable mapping**, link each template variable to a contact field or additional field. All variables must be mapped.
    3. Check the **Preview**, which shows header, body, footer, and buttons with a sample contact's data.

    **If you chose Manual text:**

    1. Write in **Message content**.
    2. Use **Variables** to insert contact data, such as Name, Email, Phone, or additional fields.
    3. Check the **Preview**, which shows how the first contact in the audience will see the message.

    <Tip>
      Templates and quick replies can include an image, video, or document. Media usually increases the read rate for offers and launches.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check **Basic information**, **Channel**, **Audience**, **Schedule**, and **Message**. Then choose:

    | Button | Result |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Save draft** | Saves without sending. You start it later from the list or the campaign page. |
    | **Save and start** | Saves and starts sending immediately. Appears when you chose **Send now**. |
    | **Save and schedule** | Saves and schedules the send for the chosen date. Appears when you chose **Schedule**. |
    | **Create campaign** | For an Ongoing Campaign, creates the campaign and activates the automation. |
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After creating an Ongoing Campaign

The Ongoing Campaign starts with no contacts. To feed the audience, open the campaign and use:

| Method | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Manual** | In the **Audience** tab, click **Add contacts**, select contacts in the preview, and choose **Add and send** or **Add and schedule**. |
| **API** | In the **Integrations** tab, copy the campaign endpoint and send contacts from your system, authenticating with `x-api-key`. See [Campaigns API →](/developers/api/campaigns). |
| **RD Station Marketing** | In the **Integrations** tab, copy the webhook and paste it into an RD Station Marketing automation. Each lead sent joins the audience. |

See [Manage campaigns →](/campaigns/manage-campaigns).

## Best practices

- Start with a small audience to validate the message before sending to your whole base.
- Schedule for business hours and avoid late nights and holidays.
- Use lower speeds on new numbers or numbers with low quality at Meta.
- Personalize with the contact's name and a relevant detail, such as the plan or product of interest.
- Prepare the AI agent and your team for the volume of replies the campaign will generate.

## Common errors

| Message | Cause | How to fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Select a channel to continue.** | No channel selected. | Select a WhatsApp channel. |
| **Select a template to continue.** | Template format without a template. | Choose a template from the list. |
| **Map all required template variables before continuing.** | Some variables have no linked field. | Complete the **Variable mapping**. |
| **Write the message content before continuing.** | Manual text format without content. | Write the message. |
| **Define the schedule date and time.** | **Schedule** without a date. | Choose the date and time. |
| **No templates available right now.** | The channel has no approved templates. | Create and get approval for a template for the channel. |
