The **Members** page has a search field and indicators that help you keep the list accurate: who has already joined, who has not accepted the invitation yet, and who owns the workspace.

<Note>
  The **Members** page is available to Administrators only.
</Note>

## Search

Type in the **Search members...** field at the top of the page. The list filters as you type, and the search is not case-sensitive.

| You type | You find |
| --- | --- |
| Part of a name | Members whose name contains the text |
| Part of an email, such as `@company.com` | Members whose email contains the text |
| `admin`, `manager`, or `member` | Members with the Administrator, Manager, or Member role, respectively |

<Info>
  Searching by role uses the internal role codes. Type `admin`, `manager`, or `member`.
</Info>

The number next to the **Members** heading shows how many people match the current search. If nothing matches, the page shows **No members found**. Clear the search text to see the full list again.

## Refresh the list

Click the **Refresh** button (circular arrow icon) to reload the data. Use it after inviting, editing, or removing someone, or when another Administrator made changes at the same time.

## Indicators in the list

| Indicator | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **You** | Your own account |
| **Owner** | The workspace owner. They cannot be removed and their role cannot change |
| **Invitation Sent** | The invitation was sent, but the person has not completed their first access. The **Resend invitation** icon is available |
| Role badge | **Administrator**, **Manager**, or **Member** in the **Role** column |

## Suggested review routine

- Search for your company domain, such as `@company.com`, to spot personal addresses that should be work emails.
- Look for people with **Invitation Sent** for many days and resend or remove the invitation.
- Search `admin` and confirm that only the right people have full access.
