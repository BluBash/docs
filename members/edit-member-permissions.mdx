sidebarTitle: "Edit role and teams"

---

After the invitation, you can promote or restrict a person's access at any time. You change the role on the **Members** page. You adjust which teams the person belongs to in the team itself.

<Note>
  Only **Administrators** can edit members.
</Note>

## What you can change and where

| What | Where | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Role** (Administrator, Manager, Member) | **Members** → pencil icon | Applies to the whole workspace |
| Teams the person belongs to | **Teams** → team → **Members** tab | The person must stay in at least one team |
| Team role (**Manager** or **Agent**) | **Teams** → team → **Members** tab | Applies only to that team |
| Name | The person's own profile | Administrators do not edit the name |

## Change the workspace role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the member">
    In the side menu, click **Members**. Use **Search members...** to find the person by name or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click the pencil icon (**Edit member**) on the person's row. The **Edit Member** window shows their name with the note **The display name is set by the user in their profile.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the new role">
    In **Role**, select **Administrator**, **Manager**, or **Member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update Member**. BluBash confirms with **Member updated successfully**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The change takes effect immediately. The person sees the **Permissions Updated** notice, and menus and actions follow the new role.

<Info>
  If you click the pencil icon on your own row, BluBash opens the **Profile** page, where you update your own details. You cannot change your own role.
</Info>

<Warning>
  The workspace **Owner** role cannot be changed. In the editor, the field is locked with the note **Owner role cannot be changed**.
</Warning>

## Adjust a member's teams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the team">
    In the side menu, click **Teams** and open the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Members tab">
    To add someone, search for the person, choose their team role (**Agent** or **Manager**), and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change or remove">
    In **Current Members**, change the team role or remove the person from the team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  BluBash does not let you remove someone from their only team in the workspace. Add the person to another team before removing them from the current one.
</Warning>

## When to change the role

| Situation | Suggested change |
| --- | --- |
| A team member now coordinates an area and needs reports and Pipelines | **Member** → **Manager** |
| A coordinator will configure AI agents, channels, and teams | **Manager** → **Administrator** |
| The person no longer needs to configure the account | **Administrator** → **Manager** or **Member** |
| A team member moved to another area | Keep the role and change the teams |

## Best practices

- Adjust teams before changing the role. Access issues are often about teams, not roles.
- Agree on the change with the person before restricting access, so conversations are not left halfway.
- Keep the number of Administrators low to protect the workspace.
