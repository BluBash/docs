title: "Roles and permissions"
description: "Learn what Administrators, Managers, and Members can do in BluBash and how teams define which conversations, contacts, and Pipelines each person can access."
---

Access in BluBash combines two things: the **workspace role** (Administrator, Manager, or Member) defines what a person can do, and the **teams** they belong to define which conversations, contacts, channels, and Pipelines they work on.

<Warning>
  Every member must belong to at least one team. Without a team, the person cannot access conversations, contacts, Pipelines, or tasks.
</Warning>

## Roles at a glance

| Role | Who it is for | Visibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Administrator** | People responsible for the account, its configuration, and the AI agents | The whole workspace |
| **Manager** | Area coordinators and supervisors | The teams they belong to |
| **Member** | Day-to-day support staff, sales reps, and analysts | The teams they belong to |

## What each role can do

| Resource | Administrator | Manager | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conversations | All | From their teams | From their teams |
| Contacts | All, including delete | From their teams, including delete | From their teams, no delete |
| Pipelines and deals | Create, configure, and delete | Create, configure, and delete | View and work their teams' Pipelines |
| Tasks | **Mine**, **Teams**, and **Workspace** scopes | **Mine** and **Teams** scopes | **Mine** scope |
| Campaigns | Yes | From their teams | No |
| Templates | Manage all | Manage, within their teams | Use |
| Tags | Manage all | Manage global tags and their teams' tags | Use |
| Reports & Insights | Yes | From their teams | No |
| AI agents | Create, train, and configure | No | No |
| Channels | Manage | Does not manage | Does not manage |
| Teams | Create, configure, and delete | Does not manage | Does not manage |
| Members | Invite, edit, and remove | No | No |
| Additional Fields | Create and edit | Fill in | Fill in |
| Billing & Usage | Yes | No | No |

<Note>
  Only Administrators create, train, and configure AI agents. Managers and Members work on the conversations the AI agent transfers to their teams and can hand a conversation back to the AI when it makes sense.
</Note>

<Info>
  A tag restricted to several teams can only be edited or deleted by someone who belongs to all of those teams. Global tags are managed by Administrators and Managers. See [Create tags →](/account-settings/create-tags).
</Info>

## Team role: Manager or Agent

Besides the workspace role, each person has a role inside every team they belong to: **Manager** or **Agent**. You set it in the team's **Members** tab. Here, "Agent" is the person who handles conversations, not the AI agent.

The team role identifies who coordinates that team and appears, for example, in the list of people when you transfer a conversation. It does not replace the workspace role: what the person can do in the platform is still defined by the Administrator, Manager, or Member role.

## Where the AI agent fits in

| Step | Who does it |
| --- | --- |
| Create, train, and configure the AI agent | Administrator |
| Answer, qualify, and transfer to a team | AI agent, through the **Transfer to Team** skill |
| Take over the transferred conversation | Members of the destination team |
| Create and move deals in Pipelines | Managers and Administrators create Pipelines; everyone works their teams' deals |
| Review quality with AI in reports | Managers and Administrators |

<Tip>
  Before transferring a conversation, leave an internal note with a summary of the case. Whoever receives it starts with the full context.
</Tip>

## How to choose each person's role

| Profile | Recommended role |
| --- | --- |
| Owners and people responsible for account and AI agent configuration | **Administrator** |
| Area coordinators and support supervisors | **Manager** |
| Day-to-day support staff, sales reps, and analysts | **Member** |

## Common issues

| Situation | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The person logs in and sees **No Team Access** | They are not in any team | Add the person to a team in the team's **Members** tab |
| A Manager cannot see a conversation | The conversation belongs to a channel or team they are not part of | Add the Manager to the responsible team |
| A Member cannot find **Campaigns** or **Reports & Insights** | These features are not part of the Member role | Change the role to Manager, if appropriate |
| The edit button for a tag is disabled | The person does not belong to all of the tag's teams | Ask an Administrator or adjust the teams |

Review roles regularly and keep the number of Administrators low.
