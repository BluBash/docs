Tags classify your contacts so the team can understand each case at a glance, filter the inbox and segment campaigns. In a conversation, tags belong to the **contact**: what you apply stays on the contact after the conversation is closed and shows up in all of their conversations.

## What tags are for

| Use | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Interest or product | "Annual plan", "Implant", "Car insurance" |
| Relationship stage | "Hot lead", "Active customer", "Overdue" |
| Source | "Referral", "Trade show 2026", "Instagram ad" |
| Special attention | "VIP", "Open complaint" |

<Tip>
  Use tags for contact characteristics. To track which stage a process is in, use [Pipelines](/pipelines/overview). Stages should not become tags.
</Tip>

## Apply tags in a conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the information panel">
    In the conversation, click the contact's name at the top or the side panel button (**Conversation Information**). The **Information** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Contact tab">
    In the **Contact** tab, scroll to the **Tags** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click +">
    The button is next to the **Tags** heading. Use **Search tags...** to find the tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the tag">
    Click the tag. It appears right away on the contact and in the conversation list. Repeat to apply more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a tag, hover over it and click the **X**. When the contact has no tags, the section shows **No tags assigned**.

## Where tags appear

- **In the conversation list**, below the name and the last message, in each tag's color.
- **On Pipeline cards**, next to the contact's deal.
- **On the contact record**, in **Contacts**.

## Filter the inbox by tag

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filters">
    In the conversation list, click **Filters**. The **More filters** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Tags">
    In the **Tags** tab, use **Search tags...** and select one or more tags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the panel">
    Changes are applied when you close the panel. To remove the filter, click **Clear**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tags applied by the AI agent

The AI agent can classify the contact on its own with the **Add Tag** and **Remove Tag** skills. For example: apply "Hot lead" when the customer asks for a quote, or remove "Overdue" when they confirm payment. That way the team can use the filter to find the contacts the AI qualified.

[Configure skills →](/ai-agents/configure-skills)

## Who creates tags

Tags are created in **Settings**, under **Tags**, with a name, description, color and availability: **Available for all teams** or **Select specific teams**. Tags restricted to some teams are only available to those teams. Only Administrators and Managers can manage global tags.

[Create tags →](/account-settings/create-tags) · [Manage contact tags →](/contacts/manage-contact-tags)

## Best practices

- Agree with the team on a short list of tags and what each one means.
- Before closing the conversation, review the contact's tags. Remove the ones that no longer apply.
- Avoid duplicate tags with similar names, such as "VIP" and "VIP customer".
- Use colors to group tags of the same kind, for example green for interest and red for attention.
