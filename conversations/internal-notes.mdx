Internal notes are comments that stay inside the conversation and only your team can see. They are never sent to the contact, on any channel. Use notes to pass on context, record agreements and keep a complete history for whoever serves the customer next.

## When to use notes

| Situation | Example note |
| --- | --- |
| Before transferring | "Customer wants to renegotiate the October installment. I already offered 10% and they declined." |
| Agreement made outside the chat | "Customer called and confirmed the technical visit for Thursday, 2 PM." |
| Manager guidance | "Prioritize this customer: contract expires this month." |
| Pending item for the team | "Waiting for finance to release the payment slip. Do not close." |

## How to add a note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    In **Conversations**, open the conversation. If it is not yours yet, click **Assign to me**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch to Note mode">
    Above the message box, click the **Message** selector and choose **Note**. The box changes color and shows **Write an internal note...**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write and send">
    Type the note and press **Enter** to send. Use **Shift+Enter** for a new line. The confirmation **Note added successfully** appears.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch back to Message mode">
    To reply to the customer, click the selector and choose **Message** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  In **Note** mode, the attach media and templates buttons are disabled. Notes are text only.
</Note>

## Where notes appear

- **In the conversation history**, between messages, in the note color that sets them apart from what the customer received.
- **In the side panel**, in the **Conversation** tab, under **Notes**. Each note shows its author and date.

| Author | How it appears |
| --- | --- |
| Team member | Name initial and the name of the person who wrote it. |
| AI agent | Sparkle icon. Notes created by the **Add Note** skill. |
| **System** | Gear icon. Automatic records from the platform. |

Some notes carry markers such as **Assignment** or **High Priority**.

## Reply to a note

Hover over the note, open the three-dot menu and click **Quote**. The message box stays in **Note** mode, quoting the original note. This keeps the internal discussion organized without sending anything to the customer.

## Notes when you cannot send messages

You can write notes even when sending messages is blocked:

- On the **WhatsApp Business API**, outside the 24-hour window, while you wait for a reply to a template.
- On **Instagram**, after 7 days without a message from the contact.

<Warning>
  While the AI agent is handling the conversation, the message box is replaced by the **AI Agent Active** notice. To write a note, click **Take Over** first.
</Warning>

## AI agent notes

With the **Add Note** skill, the AI agent records summaries and observations during the conversation, for example the data collected during qualification. Configure the agent to leave a note before transferring. That way, the team member starts out knowing what was already discussed.

[Configure skills →](/ai-agents/configure-skills)

## Best practices

- Write for someone who does not know the case: what the customer wants, what was done and what is missing.
- Record agreed dates and amounts. They are the reference if there is a disagreement.
- Do not record passwords, card data or full ID documents in notes.
- Conversation notes stay with the conversation. To record something that follows the process, use **Note** on the Pipeline deal.
