Quick replies are ready-made messages your team uses every day: introduction, opening question, payment methods, address, business hours. They save typing and keep the same communication standard across the whole team.

## Quick reply or template?

|  | Quick reply | Template |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta approval | Not required | Required |
| When to use | During the conversation, when sending messages is allowed | To start a conversation or resume after 24 hours on the WhatsApp Business API |
| Content | Text, image, video, document or contacts | Format defined by Meta |
| Marker in the list | **Quick Reply** | **Template** |

## How to send a quick reply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    In **Conversations**, open the conversation. It must be assigned to you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the lightning button">
    The button is below the message box, next to the attach button. Hover over it to see **Templates**: **Use template or quick reply**. The **Select Template** window opens with the description **Templates and quick replies for use in conversation**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the quick reply">
    Use **Search templates...** to search by name or by a word in the text. Templates appear first; quick replies come next, marked **Quick Reply**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the variables, if any">
    Variables such as `{{1}}` and `{{2}}` personalize the text. The first one is already filled with the contact's name, when available. For the others, type the value or pick contact data from **Basic Fields**, **Additional Information** or **Additional Fields**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the preview and send">
    Review the text in **Message Preview** and click **Send Template**. The same button sends templates and quick replies. You can also press **Enter**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The message appears in the conversation like any other message you sent.

**Example saved as a quick reply:**

```text
Hi {{1}}, this is {{2}} and I'll be taking over your request.

How can I help you today?
```

**After filling in the variables:**

```text
Hi Maria, this is Ana and I'll be taking over your request.

How can I help you today?
```

## When quick replies are not available

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Note** mode on | The lightning button is disabled. Switch back to **Message**. |
| WhatsApp Business API outside the 24-hour window | The window only lists templates. Send a template and wait for the contact to reply. |
| Instagram after 7 days without a message from the contact | Sending is blocked until the contact writes again. |
| AI agent handling the conversation | The message box is locked. Click **Take Over** first. |

## Best practices

- Use short, searchable names, such as "pix payment" or "downtown store address".
- Use variables for the contact's and team member's names. The message feels personal with no extra typing.
- Check the preview before sending. A wrong variable reaches the customer.
- Keep a small, up-to-date set of quick replies. Remove the ones nobody uses.
