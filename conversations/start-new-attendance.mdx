The **Conversations** screen brings WhatsApp, Instagram and Webchat messages together in a single inbox. On this page you learn how to find conversations, take the ones that reached you or are with the AI, and start a new conversation with a contact.

## Get to know the inbox

### Quick filters

At the top of the list, quick filters split conversations by situation. On narrow screens, some of them move to **More types**.

| Filter | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every conversation you can see. |
| **Mine** | Conversations assigned to you. |
| **Queue** | Conversations waiting for someone to take them. |
| **AI** | Conversations handled by an AI agent. |
| **Unread** | Conversations with messages nobody has read. |
| **Closed** | Conversations already closed, for reference. |

### Search and advanced filters

- Use the **Search conversations...** field to find a specific conversation.
- Click **Filters** to open **More filters** and combine **Channel**, **Team**, **Member** and **Tags**. Active filters are highlighted below the search. Use **Clear** to remove them.

### What each list item shows

| Signal | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Icon on the corner of the photo | The conversation's channel: WhatsApp, Instagram or Webchat. Hover to see the channel name. |
| Sparkle icon next to the name | **Hybrid conversation (user + AI)**: the conversation has a team member and an AI agent. |
| Alert icon and highlighted background | **Transferred to you - requires attention** or **Pending - awaiting your action**. |
| Archive icon | The conversation is closed. |
| Colored tags | The contact's tags. |
| Highlighted number | Unread messages. |

## Reply to incoming conversations

### Pending conversation or one transferred to you

When nobody has taken the conversation yet, the message box shows **This conversation needs to be assigned**. Click **Assign to me**. The conversation becomes yours and the message box is unlocked.

### Conversation handled by the AI agent

If the channel has an AI agent, it answers first. Meanwhile, the message box is replaced by the **AI Agent Active** notice, with the text "agent name **is managing this conversation**".

To talk to the customer:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Take Over">
    The button is in the **AI Agent Active** notice, in place of the message box.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the reason (optional)">
    In the **Take Over** window, describe why you are taking over, for example "customer asked for a discount".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Take Over**. The agent stops replying and the message box is unlocked for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the human part is done, you can hand the conversation back to the AI with **Handoff to AI**.

## Start a new conversation

Start a conversation when you need to reach out to the customer first: a quote follow-up, a payment reminder, a heads-up. The contact must already exist. If not, [add the contact](/contacts/add-new-contact) first.

### Which channels let you start a conversation

| Channel | Can you start? | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp Business API** | Yes | The first message must be a Meta-approved template. |
| **WhatsApp Lite** | Yes | You send a free-form message. |
| **Instagram** | No | Receptive only: the conversation starts when the contact sends a Direct message. |
| **Webchat** | No | Receptive only: the visitor starts it from the website widget. |

### Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Conversations">
    Click **Conversations** in the side menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the + button">
    The button is at the top of the list, next to the refresh button. The **Start New Conversation** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the contact">
    In **Contact**, type at least 3 characters of the name, phone or email and select the contact from the results. To switch, click **Change contact**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the team">
    In **Team**, select the team responsible for the conversation. If you belong to only one team, it is already selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the channel">
    In **Channel**, select the WhatsApp number. The list only shows the channels of the selected team. Instagram and Webchat channels appear disabled, marked **Receptive only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Start Conversation">
    The conversation is created and opened on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the first message">
    On the **WhatsApp Business API**, BluBash shows the **Start WhatsApp conversation** notice and the **Choose template** button. Send an approved template. After the contact replies, you continue with free-form messages. On **WhatsApp Lite**, write normally.

    [Send a template →](/conversations/send-templates)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If a conversation with the contact is already in progress on the same channel, BluBash does not create another one: you are taken to the existing conversation with the **Redirected to existing conversation** notice.
</Info>

### Other ways to start

- **From the contact record**: use the conversation action on the contact. [Start a conversation from a contact →](/contacts/start-conversation-from-contact)
- **From a closed conversation**: open the conversation and click **Start new conversation**. BluBash creates the conversation on the same channel, preferably with the same team.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The + button does not appear">
    You need to belong to at least one active team. Ask an Administrator to add you to the right team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The message You cannot initiate conversations in this channel appears">
    The team or the channel does not allow you to start conversations. Ask the Administrator to review your teams and the **Allow initiating conversations** option in the team settings. On receptive-only channels, such as Instagram and Webchat, only the contact starts the conversation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No channels available for this team appears">
    The selected team has no linked channel. Choose another team or ask the Administrator to link a channel to it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The contact does not appear in the search">
    Type at least 3 characters and check the spelling or the number. If the contact does not exist, add it in **Contacts**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
