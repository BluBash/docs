The **History** is the contact's timeline. It shows, in chronological order, every recorded event: who changed the profile, which tags were added or removed, how deals moved through Pipelines, which tasks were created, and which campaigns the contact received.

Use the history to understand the context before replying, audit changes, and see what the AI agent did during the conversation.

## Open the history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    In the sidebar, click **Contacts** and then click the contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the History tab">
    The **History** tab opens by default in the center of the page. The most recent events appear first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to see older events">
    When you scroll to the end, BluBash loads earlier events automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the contact has no events yet, **No history recorded yet** appears.

## What each event shows

| Information | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Icon and color** | Identify the event type, such as tag, deal, task, or campaign. |
| **Description** | What happened, for example **Tag "vip" added** or **Deal "Annual plan" moved from Proposal to Negotiation**. |
| **Author** | Who performed the action: a user, an AI agent, the API, an integration, an automation, or the system. |
| **Date and time** | When the event happened, in your time zone. |
| **Details** | Changed fields, source and destination stages, or delivery status, when available. |

## Event types

| Category | Events |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | **Contact created**, **Contact updated**, **Contact added via import**, **Contact updated via import**, **Contact merged** |
| **Owners** | **Owner change**, with the global or team owner before and after |
| **Tags** | **Tag added**, **Tag removed** |
| **Deals** | **Deal created**, **Deal moved**, **Deal won**, **Deal lost**, **Deal updated** |
| **Tasks** | **Task created**, **Task completed**, **Task reopened**, **Task updated**, **Task removed** |
| **Campaigns** | **Campaign sent**, **Campaign delivered**, **Campaign read**, **Campaign failed** |
| **Messages and notes** | Messages received and sent per channel, **Note added** |
| **Automations and integrations** | **Automation triggered**, **Automation completed**, **Automation failed**, **Integration sync**, **Integration error** |

## AI agent actions in the history

In hybrid service, the history shows the AI agent's work side by side with your team's. When the agent updates contact data or applies a tag, the event appears with the agent's name as the author.

This lets you:

- Check whether the agent collected the right data before the transfer.
- Audit tags applied automatically.
- Adjust the training when the agent saves information in the wrong field.

See [Hybrid service →](/ai-agents/hybrid-service).

## The other contact tabs

| Tab | What it shows | Available action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Conversations** | The contact's conversations, with status and a preview of the last message. The number in parentheses shows open conversations. | **Create a conversation** |
| **Deals** | Pipelines and stages for this contact, with amount and status (Open, Won, Lost, or Archived). | **Create a deal** |
| **Tasks** | The contact's tasks, with type (Reminder, Call, or Meeting), due date, and an optional link to a deal. | **Create a task** |

<Tip>
  Before taking a conversation transferred by the AI agent, open the contact's **History**. In a few seconds you see the open deals, the latest tags, and what has already been agreed.
</Tip>

## Limitations

- The history records events from the moment the related feature started being used. Actions taken outside BluBash appear only if they arrive through an integration or the API.
- When the contact is deleted, the history is deleted with it and cannot be recovered.
