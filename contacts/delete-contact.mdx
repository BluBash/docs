Deleting a contact removes the record and all data linked to it. Use this action to remove duplicate or test records, or contacts who asked for their data to be removed.

<Warning>
  Deletion is **permanent and cannot be undone**. The contact's conversations, deals, and history are also removed and no longer appear in reports.
</Warning>

## Who can delete

| Role | Can delete |
| --- | --- |
| **Administrator** | Yes |
| **Manager** | Yes, on contacts of their teams |
| **Member** | No. The option does not appear. |

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    In the sidebar, click **Contacts** and then click the contact you want to delete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the options menu">
    In the contact header, next to the name, click **More options** (three-dot icon).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete Contact">
    Click **Delete Contact**. The **Delete Contact** confirmation window opens, showing the contact's name and email or phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the affected data">
    The window lists the **Data that will be deleted**. Make sure this is the right contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete Contact**. BluBash shows **Contact deleted successfully** and returns to the contact list. To give up, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What is removed

| Data | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversations** | The contact's conversations are removed. |
| **Conversation History** | The exchanged messages no longer exist. |
| **Contact Data** | Name, email, phone, WhatsApp, additional fields, tags, and owners. |
| **Deals** | Deals linked to the contact in Pipelines are removed. |
| **Files** | Media and documents linked to the contact. |
| **Contact Reports** | The contact's data no longer counts in reports. |
| **Contact Activities** | The entire **History**, including tasks and events. |

## Before deleting

- **Duplicate contact**: check which record has the most complete conversations and deals. Delete the other one.
- **Inactive customer**: prefer keeping the contact and applying a tag, such as `inactive`. This preserves the history and metrics.
- **Open deals**: mark deals as won or lost first if you want to keep the result in reports. Deleting the contact also removes its deals.
- **Data removal request**: deleting is the right path when the data subject asks for their personal data to be erased.

## Common errors

| Message | Cause | How to fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cannot delete contact that has active conversations** | The contact has an ongoing conversation. | Close the active conversations and try again. See [Close a conversation →](/conversations/close-attendance). |
| **Error deleting contact** | Temporary failure or missing permission. | Check your role and try again. |
| The **Delete Contact** option does not appear | Your role is Member. | Ask a Manager or Administrator to delete it. |

<Note>
  If the contact sends a new message after being deleted, BluBash creates a new contact, without the previous history.
</Note>
